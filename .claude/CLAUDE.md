# Austin Music Video Film Festival

Post-event marketing site for the Austin Music Video Film Festival, held July 18, 2026, at Cabana Club in East Austin. Features video hero with first-visit loader, typewriter tagline, parallax starfield backdrop, partner marquee, program schedule, venue details, FAQ accordion, team roster, event photo gallery with lightbox backed by a content collection, contact directory, store link to the shop subdomain, and custom 404 and 500 pages. Built with Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and GSAP. Deployed on Netlify.
