# Austin Music Video Film Festival

Post-event marketing site for the Austin Music Video Film Festival, held July 18, 2026, at Cabana Club. Features video hero, program schedule, FAQ, team roster, an event photo gallery backed by a content collection, and contact directory. Built with Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and GSAP, with a dormant Supabase CLI scaffold. Deployed on Netlify.
